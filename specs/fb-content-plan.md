## Suggested Posting Order

Start broad and educational, move toward specific use cases, finish with conversion-focused posts as you build a warm audience for your Meta contact to retarget.

1. Post 1 (explainer — what it is)
2. Post 2 (volunteer experience — what the link looks like)
3. Post 3 or 4 (use case — sports)
4. Post 7 (confirmation email — builds trust)
5. Post 5 (use case — non-sports, broadens audience)
6. Post 6 (use case — schools)
7. Post 8 (feature — spots still needed)
8. Post 9 (feature — roster/export)
9. Post 10 (conversion CTA — retargeting)

---

## Notes for Meta boost targeting

- **Posts 3–6** each target a distinct audience segment — your Meta contact can boost each to a matching interest group rather than blasting the same ad to everyone
- **Post 7 (confirmation email)** is a strong trust-builder and works well as a retargeting ad for people who visited the site but didn't sign up
- **Install the Meta Pixel** before boosting Post 10 — you need it to build a retargeting audience from site visitors
- Once you have any real user signups, a lookalike audience built from your actual organizer emails will outperform interest targeting
